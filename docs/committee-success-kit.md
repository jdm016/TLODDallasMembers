## Part 1: What Every Chair and Co-Chair Is Expected To Do

These seven expectations apply to every committee. They come straight from what our chapter told us at the retreat.

1. **Own a Program of Work.** Plan your committee's activities for the year, aligned to the chapter calendar and our mission, and build it using the national PIVOT method (Part 2). Submit it on the chapter's Program of Work Submission Form.
2. **Budget your work.** Give the Finance Committee your anticipated needs, and operate within your approved line.
3. **Communicate to the chapter.** This is our number one gap. Give a brief monthly update, and announce activities with enough lead time that no Lady is surprised.
4. **Report and document.** File a post-event report after every activity, and send records and photos to Awards and Archives. This is how our service counts toward 5-Star recognition. If it is not reported, it did not happen.
5. **Recruit and involve your members.** Pull your committee members in, share the load, and build bench strength. Retention is our universal threat, and involvement is the cure.
6. **Partner with your co-chair.** Split the work, cover each other, and keep continuity so no seat goes dark. Leadership succession is a live risk this year.
7. **Follow the bylaws and protocol.** Honor chapter traditions, deadlines, and TLOD guidelines in everything your committee does.

## Part 2: The Chair's Operating Rhythm

Plan, then fund, then execute, then report, then reflect. Here is the year at a glance.

| When | What every chair does |
| --- | --- |
| **September** | Submit your Program of Work. Confirm your budget line with Finance. Introduce your committee and its plan to the chapter. |
| **Monthly** | Deliver a brief chapter update (done / next / need). Execute your planned activities. |
| **After each activity** | File a post-event report within one week. Send photos to Archives and Social Media. |
| **Quarterly** | Check your progress against your Program of Work; adjust as needed. |
| **January** | Contribute your committee's input to the mid-year SWOT & Ready progress pulse. |
| **June (Founders' Day)** | Close out the year, submit final reports, and hand your files to your successor within 30 days (per bylaws). |

**Plan with PIVOT.** TLOD plans and runs every program and project with PIVOT. Use it to shape your Program of Work and your after-action reflection:

- **P, Plan** programs and projects with and through partnerships.
- **I, Implement** innovative steps to complete them.
- **V, Value** the service you give, and validate it via surveys.
- **O, Operations**, outreach, and occurrences, noting changes (additions or deletions).
- **T, Timing and Teamwork** that keep partnerships sustainable.

**Why your reporting matters: the 5-Star Chapter Recognition Program.** Our chapter earns national recognition (Orchid, Emerald, Sapphire, Ruby, Diamond) based on what we do and what we can prove. Your reports feed it. Recognition rewards:

- **Chapter operations:** joint meetings and community service with Top Teens, the annual joint retreat, Founders' Day and Rededication, Form 990 filed by August 1, and monthly meetings with opening and closing ceremonies.
- Including **3 to 5 of the five program thrusts** (Top Teens of America required).
- Including **3 to 5 national projects** (2025-2027 National Projects: NCNW, NAACP, When We All Vote, Legislative Action, UNCF, TLOD Literacy, and Sickle Cell Disease Awareness).
- Including **1 to 3 national program topics** (for example Domestic Violence, Suicide Prevention, Human Trafficking, Alzheimer's, Heart Disease and Stroke, Lupus, Mental Health, Eye Care).

The more of your work you document and report, the higher we rise.

### A3. The Chapter Mentorship Program (Two Tracks)

Mentorship serves both sides of our mission at once, so we run it as one program with two tracks. Plan each with PIVOT, and report pairings and activities like any other program of work.

**Me and My Mentor (Teens)** - owned by Service to Youth with the Lords.
- Pair every Top Teen with a Top Lady mentor (and a Lord where possible).
- Complete a Mentor/Mentee Profile Sheet for each pair.
- Hold quarterly mentoring activities and benchmarks.
- Gather at TLC (Teens and Ladies Connecting) culminating events: luncheons, service projects, cultural outings, worship, and team-building.
- Submit a profile/activity sheet to National for the Me and My Mentor publication.
- **Why it matters:** this is the national Me and My Mentor program, and it lifts our Leadership Pipeline.

**Sisterly Mentorship (Ladies)** - owned by Membership and Sisterly Relations.
- Pair seasoned Ladies with new, reinstated, and emerging-leader Ladies.
- Guide onboarding, deepen sisterhood, and build our officer and committee bench.
- Check in monthly, and celebrate pairs at chapter gatherings.
- **Why it matters:** this is a direct lever on our retention threat, and it lifts Volunteer Strength and Unity.

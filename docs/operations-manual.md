## How to use this manual

This manual is how the Dallas Chapter runs from year to year, so any Lady stepping into a role knows what to do, when, and where to find it. It sits alongside the TLOD, Inc. national Constitution and Bylaws and never replaces them; where the two differ, national governs.

- **Who it is for:** every Lady of the chapter, with extra detail for officers, committee chairs, and Top Teens of America advisors.
- **Where it lives:** linked from the Member Portal under Resources, so there is one current copy.
- **How it changes:** any Lady can suggest an edit through a comment or the portal's feedback form. The Executive Board reviews suggestions quarterly and adopts changes by vote.
- **Annual review:** the incoming President and Secretary review the full manual at officer transition (June to July) and update names, dates, and the chapter calendar.

Items marked **Confirm** below are places where chapter or national practice should be checked and filled in before the manual is adopted.

### Program lifecycle

1. **Plan:** each thrust and committee submits its biennium Program of Work (goals, planned events and partnerships, resources needed, success measures) through the Program of Work Submission Form. For 2025-2027 these were due to the 1st Vice President on October 21, 2025.
2. **Consolidate:** the 1st Vice President submits the chapter Program of Work to National (October 23, 2025 for this biennium).
3. **Schedule:** chairs put dates on the chapter calendar and the portal Projects tracker, with a lead and status.
4. **Deliver:** run the event; collect attendance, photos, and service hours, including planning, setup, and travel time.
5. **Report:** submit hours, attendance, photos, and a summary within 7 days; the Post-Event Evaluation within 30 days (preferred by the 1st of the month, grace through the 5th).
6. **Review:** the Program Committee meets monthly to check reporting, collaboration, and progress toward biennium goals.

### Project statuses

Use the same five statuses everywhere: **Idea, Planning, In progress, Complete, On hold.**

## Membership

Every new Lady has a sponsor who brings her in and a separate mentor who walks with her through her first years. Recent induction classes were held in November 2023, October 2024, and February 2025, and a new class paid induction fees in August 2025.

### From interest to induction

1. A prospective member is sponsored by a Lady in good standing.
2. The Membership Committee (Lady Stephanie Edwards, Chair) and the 2nd Vice President confirm eligibility.
3. The candidate pays the induction fee ($800 in 2025) to the Financial Secretary by Zelle, check, cashier's check, money order, or cash, with a receipt.
4. Orientation on TLOD history, protocol, chapter programs, and this manual.
5. The Induction Committee (Lady Orethann Price, Chair) plans the ceremony according to TLOD ritual, with the Protocol Committee.
6. The new Lady is added to the directory, GroupMe, Drive, and portal within 14 days, and chooses a committee on the Committee Sign-Up Sheet.
7. The 1st Vice President pairs her with a mentor.

### Ladies Mentorship Program

- Each Lady completes the Ladies Mentorship Assessment Form as mentor, mentee, or both.
- Forms go to the 1st Vice President, who makes the matches.
- A mentor is always someone other than the Lady's sponsor.
- Matched pairs connect at least once a month for the program term.

### Good standing

A Lady is in good standing when dues and assessments are current and she meets national and chapter participation requirements (Confirm exact requirements, such as attendance and service hours). The Financial Secretary keeps the payment record; the Membership Committee keeps participation.

### Care and connection

- **Care, Condolences and Compassion Form:** any Lady can alert the chapter to an illness, bereavement, or celebration; the Devotion and Courtesy and Gifts Committees follow up.
- **Sisterly Relations:** builds connection and upholds chapter traditions.
- **Partner organizations:** Ladies record other memberships (such as NAACP) in the Member Information workbook so partnership chairs can coordinate.

### Directory upkeep

The 2025-2027 Chapter Directory (master with photos) is the single source for member contact details. Ladies send changes to the Membership Committee, which updates the directory within 7 days.

## Top Teens of America

The Dallas TTA chapter has 27 teens on the 2026 roster. Teens meet every 4th Sunday from 3:00 to 5:00 PM, and every Top Teen is paired with a TLOD mentor through the Me and My Mentor program.

- **Oversight:** the Service to Youth thrust oversees the Top Teens Program of Work under national TTA guidelines. TTA leads for 2026 are Lady LaSheryl Walker and Lady Tiara Tyler (Confirm advisor titles). The chapter 1st Vice President mentors the TTA 1st Vice President.
- **Roster:** teen and parent contacts, school, grade, and date joined, kept by the TTA advisor. Updated after each TTA induction (recent classes: March 2025 and December 2025) and each August for grade changes.
- **Signature events:** TTA Induction Ceremony and Etiquette Workshop (December 7, 2025, 3:00 to 5:00 PM); Blue and Silver Ball (December 13, 2025, with practices from late October at St. Philip's); TTA Speak-Off; Joint TLOD/TTA Retreat.
- **Scholarship:** the Scholarship Committee awards an annual scholarship to a college-bound Top Teen.
- **Lords:** the Lords Committee invites the Lords to encourage the Teens, especially the young men.
- **Reporting:** teen events are reported under both TLOD and TTA for credit; only TLOD and TTA member hours count toward chapter totals.
- **Mentor pairing:** teens and a parent or guardian complete the TTA Teen Mentorship Assessment Form; the 1st Vice President makes matches.

### Youth safety standards

- [ ] Signed parent or guardian permission on file for every teen before any activity
- [ ] At least two adults present at every teen activity; no one-on-one private settings
- [ ] Mentor contact with teens happens by group text or with a parent copied
- [ ] Medical release and emergency contact carried by the lead advisor at every event
- [ ] Confirm: national youth protection policy and any background check requirement for advisors and mentors

Teen contact details are never posted on the portal or in public channels; they stay in the access-controlled roster.

## Finance

Two people touch every dollar: the Financial Secretary receives and records it, and the Treasurer deposits and disburses it, so the books reconcile every month.

### How money flows

1. **Receive:** members pay the Financial Secretary (Lady Shenequa C. Miller) by Zelle, check, cashier's check, money order, or cash. Each payment is logged with date, payer, amount, method, reference number, and reason.
2. **Hand off:** the Financial Secretary delivers checks and cash to the Treasurer (Lady Tallulah Matthews) with a tally that notes Zelle payments already in the account.
3. **Deposit and pay:** the Treasurer deposits funds and makes approved payments.
4. **Report:** the Financial Secretary presents a monthly financial summary at the chapter meeting (for example, the August 2025 summary on September 18); the Treasurer presents the treasurer's report and bank statements.

### Accounts

The chapter keeps four bank accounts by purpose: Ladies (operating), Teens (TTA), Juanita M. Johns (luncheon), and Savings. Statements are reviewed monthly and filed in the Finance folder. Account numbers stay in the Finance folder only.

### Budget and controls

- **Budget:** the Finance Committee (Lady Tunia Hughes, Chair; Lady Beverly Randall, Co-Chair; Lady Lois Smith, Advisor) presents the next year's budget at the September chapter meeting; the chapter votes (the 2025-2026 budget was voted September 18, 2025).
- **Approvals:** every expenditure lists who approved it, the purpose, the payee, the account it is paid from, and a receipt (the Officer/Committee Expenditures log in the Finance Tracker).
- **Reimbursements:** submit a receipt within 30 days; paid only for approved items (Confirm threshold that needs Executive Board approval).
- **Event finance:** each major event has a finance lead (the Treasurer for the 2026 retreat) for budget tracking and reimbursements.
- **Year-end:** the Finance Tracker includes audit and financial review prep; filings to national and the IRS follow the national calendar (Confirm the chapter's IRS filing type).
- **Records:** statements, receipts, and reports stay in the Finance folder for 7 years.
